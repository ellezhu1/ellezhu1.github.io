All trees start as small fallen seeds,

Even great oaks and wide redwoods.

Once each seed is snug in the ground,

It sprouts into a tiny sapling!


Each sapling grows and grows and grows

And now they are mature grown trees!

What now awaits these trees one wonders?

Of course, it's the life of a tree!


In the sizzling sweaty summer,

The trees spread their rich green leaves.

Their leaves are like an unbrella,

Shading the tired, hot humans from above.


In the chilly relaxing autumn,

The trees' leaves turn red, orange, and yellow.

Then they fall softly drifting like the time.

They are glass under heavy children's feet.


In the cool beauty of winter,

many lovely and leafless trees

Fall into a deep sleep dreamming,

While wrapped in a coat of white snow.


In the lively happy spring,

The hibernating bare tress yawn and awaken.

The rough barked trees are ready

To blossom and show their beauty!
